# Глосарій: українська (uk)

The Ukrainian translation of each [glossary](glossary.md) term, used every time the term appears
in the `uk.toml` catalogs. Neither DaVinci Resolve nor Premiere Pro ships a Ukrainian interface,
so the Kdenlive and Shotcut columns record what those Ukrainian localizations say (not checked
against a running copy: "not confirmed"). Where jvid picks a different word, the note says why:
mostly the term Ukrainian editors use day to day, which is often the Resolve/Premiere loan word.
See [Translating jvid](../translating.md).

## Conventions

- **Spelling**: the 2019 orthography («проєкт»).
- **Capitalization**: sentence case for labels, buttons, menus, tabs and headings ("Зберегти як",
  "Черга рендерингу"), as in Ukrainian Windows and macOS.
- **Buttons and menu items** use the infinitive ("Зберегти", "Скасувати", "Експортувати").
  Hints addressed to the user use the polite imperative ("Перетягніть кліпи сюди").
- **Quotation marks**: «ялинки». **Apostrophe**: ’ (U+2019); the bundled fonts don't draw ʼ.
- **Units**: МБ, ГБ, КБ, Б, дБ, с, пкс, кадр/с, with a space before the unit. LUFS, codec and
  format names, keycaps and shortcut notation stay as written.
- **No abbreviations to fit a width**: long labels are the layout's job (#310).
- **Dates**: day before month, CLDR's abbreviated months ("24 жовт.", "24 жовт. 2026 р.").
- **Plurals**: `one` (1, 21…), `few` (2–4, 22–24…), `many` (0, 5–20, 25…), and `other`
  (fractions only, never shown) in the genitive singular.
