# Chinese (Simplified) glossary (简体中文)

the Simplified Chinese DaVinci Resolve or Premiere Pro interface; where those two disagree, the note
says which one jvid follows. Use the one word below every time the term appears; see
[Translating jvid](../translating.md). Terms marked *unconfirmed* were not checked against a

## Conventions

- **No capitalization**: labels and buttons are short noun or verb-object phrases: 保存, 新建项目,
  添加标记, 导出帧. Questions end in ？ (要删除 {file} 吗？).
- **Punctuation**: full-width ，。？：；“” in Chinese text; a name the user gave (project, clip,
  effect, section) is quoted “{name}”, a `{path}` or `{file}` stays bare. The ellipsis … stays as
  English has it. Parentheses are half-width with a leading space (重做 (备用), 系统 ({language})).
- **Spaces**: a half-width space between Chinese and a Latin word, number or placeholder that holds
  one (`{count} 个片段`, `PNG 图像`); none around a quoted name.
- **Counters**: clips and exports 个, items in the queue 项; Chinese has only the `other` plural form.
- **Dates**: `{month}` is 1月…12月, so 今年 is `{month}{day}日` and other years
  `{year}年{month}{day}日`.
