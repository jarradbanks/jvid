Graphic EQ and Presets

Mockups for #263: a 10-band graphic EQ (31 Hz to 16 kHz, ±15 dB, plus Post-Gain) with presets, and the same presets for the Compressor. Every EQ on this page works: drag a band, double-click it to reset, pick a preset.

The Inspector is 288px wide at 1280×720 (the default_dock() split) and 240px at the 960×600 minimum window, which leaves 260px and 214px for content. Ten bands have to fit in 214px, so each band column is 21px at the minimum.

What's wrong now

  • Seven rows of sliders for three bands. Frequency and Q are numbers to type, and nothing shows the shape they make.
  • Gain runs ±24 dB, so most of each slider's travel is spent on moves nobody makes on voice or game audio.
  • No presets: cleaning up a mic means setting seven values by ear every time, and the Compressor's defaults (Ratio 1:1) do nothing until you know which four values to change.
  • The track's Audio Effects is a 320px submenu of the track menu, so it closes when the pointer crosses another menu item.

Three directions

All three sit in the Inspector's Audio tab at its default 288px, with the EQ then the Compressor as today, and the same Preset row. Each starts on Voice; drag to compare.

States, in direction A

The recommended direction at both widths. Dragged bands turn Light Blue with their value; a manual edit turns the preset to Custom. The bypassed block keeps its settings, greyed, as today.

Track Popover

The track header's Audio Effects item stops being a submenu. It opens a 320px popover beside the track header with the same EQ and Compressor blocks, which stays open until a click outside it or Escape, so a drag can wander.

Preset values

Starting values for the build to tune by ear. The dropdown names the preset whose values match exactly, and shows Custom otherwise, so no preset name is stored in the project.

Recommendation: A, Slider Bank

  • It is the widget #263 describes, and the one editors and players already know: one slider per band, value above, frequency below, curve behind.
  • It fits. Every band is a full-height column, 21px wide at the 240px minimum and 26px at the default, so the whole column is the hit target, not an 8px node. At the minimum, 125, 250 and 500 sit about 3px apart in 10px type with proportional digits; tight, but every band keeps its label. B's nodes are the same columns with smaller targets.
  • It keeps every value visible without a click. C saves 110px of height but costs a click per adjustment, in a tab that already scrolls little once it holds only Audio.
  • One widget, two places. The Inspector block and the 320px track popover draw the same graphic_eq(ui, &mut bands); the popover just gets 296px of width.
  • Build notes: 0.5 dB steps; Shift-drag for 0.1 dB; double-click resets a band; Up and Down nudge the hovered band. Grid lines at 0 (Border Strong) and ±6, ±12 (Border); the plot edges are ±15. A drag is one undo step, and a preset is one undo step.
